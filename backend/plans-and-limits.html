Plans, limits and update delivery

The hosted Native Update service has three plans. Free includes every feature with 30 apps and 5,000 monthly active users; Pro and Team raise the limits. Current prices and limits are on the pricing page. A monthly active user is a distinct device that checked for updates in the calendar month.

This page is about the hosted service only. A server you run yourself never sends any of this.

When an account goes over its limit​

Once a day the service compares each account with its plan's app and monthly-active-user limits.

From the first day over:

  • New uploads, promotions and wider rollouts are refused with HTTP 402 and the code PLAN_LIMIT. You can still narrow or pause a rollout.
  • Devices keep receiving the release they are on, and any rollout already in progress.
  • You get an email, and the billing page shows the date delivery pauses.

After 7 days over the limit: update delivery pauses. Devices are told that an update exists, but no bundle is served until you upgrade or get back under the limit. Your apps keep running the bundle they have. Delivery resumes on the next check after the account is back within its plan.

A paid plan that ends returns the account to Free. Nothing is deleted. If the account is then over the Free limits, the same steps apply.

What your app's users see​

From plugin 4.2.0, checkForUpdate() and getLatest() report the pause, so you can tell your users why the update they were promised has not arrived:

const result = await NativeUpdate.checkForUpdate();

if (result.held) {
  // A newer release (result.latestVersion) exists; delivery is paused on the developer's account.
  showBanner(result.heldMessage);
}

available stays false while the update is held, so nothing is downloaded. Older plugin versions read the same answer as "no update". The wire shape is in the API contract.
