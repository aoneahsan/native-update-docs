# Support Native Update

If Native Update has saved you a store review or two, you can help keep it going at
[nativeupdate.aoneahsan.com/support](https://nativeupdate.aoneahsan.com/support).

- **Once or monthly.** Choose either.
- **Your price.** Pick a suggested amount or name your own, from $1.
- **No account needed.** Anyone can support the project, signed in or not.
- **It is a gift.** It does not change your plan. Plans are on the [pricing page](https://nativeupdate.aoneahsan.com/pricing).

Payments are handled by Polar, the merchant of record. The amount you choose opens on Polar's checkout, where you
can still change it before paying.

It pays for the update servers, the bandwidth every rollout uses, and the time it takes to keep the plugin current
with each Capacitor release.
