# Referrals

**Dashboard → Referrals** holds your referral link, with **Copy link** and **Share**.

## What counts

A referral is **confirmed** when the person you invited has created an account **and** verified their email.
A click, or a sign-up that never verifies, does not count. The page shows three counts:

- **Confirmed** — account created and email verified.
- **Pending** — signed up, email not verified yet.
- **Void** — removed or not allowed.

## What you earn

Today, each confirmed referral earns **10 points** and a dated *Referral* badge, and every **3** confirmed
referrals earn **1 month of Pro**, up to **6 months a year**. A month earned this way extends a plan you already
pay for. It never replaces it.

These numbers are set by the Native Update team and may change. Your Referrals page always shows the current
ones, and its **Reward history** lists every reward you received.
