# Native Update for developers

**Everything you do to a release in the dashboard can also be done from a script, from an AI app, or answered by your own server the moment it happens.** Upload a bundle from CI, ask Claude how far 4.2.1 has rolled out, or post to your team chat when a build fails its checks. All three are set up in the dashboard's **Developer area**: **Access tokens**, **AI connections** and **Webhooks**.

Each one reaches only the apps your own account owns. Apps you work on through a team stay out of reach; their owner sets these up for them.

## Three ways in {#three-ways-in}

| You want to | Use | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Upload, publish, promote or roll out from CI or a script | An access token (`nu_pat_…`) and the Public Management API | [Access tokens](/developers/access-tokens), [The API](/developers/api) |
| Ask an AI app about releases, or have it change a rollout | An AI connection over MCP, with the permissions you tick | [AI apps](/developers/assistants) |
| Hear about each upload, rollout change or key change on your server | A webhook address with a signing secret | [Webhooks](/developers/webhooks) |

## What guards all three {#guards}

- **Secrets are shown again only right after a sign-in.** A token, an app key or a webhook signing secret is shown again only within 10 minutes of a Google sign-in, and with a code from your authenticator app when two-step sign-in is on. [Two-step sign-in](/developers/two-step-sign-in) explains both.
- **Deletes are opt-in.** An access token deletes nothing unless you ticked a delete permission for it. An AI app can only ask to delete; you say yes in Native Update.
- **Each has its own place on your plan.** The Developer area shows your use against the plan's limits: access tokens, API requests a day (reset at 00:00 UTC), webhook addresses and AI connections. The numbers per plan are on the [pricing page](https://nativeupdate.aoneahsan.com/pricing/).
