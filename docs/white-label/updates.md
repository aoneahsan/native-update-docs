# Updates and renewals

An update is every Native Update release after yours: new plugin versions, server releases and dashboard changes.
We apply them to your deployment; your customers pick up a new plugin version when they upgrade the package.

- **Included.** A license or a buyout includes six months of updates from the day you pay.
- **Renewals.** A renewal adds 3, 6 or 12 months, counted from the later of today and the day your updates end.
  Renew on the [updates page](https://nativeupdate.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates) with your license key and the
  email it was issued to.
- **Without a renewal.** Your deployment keeps running on the release it has. Nothing is switched off.

Renewal prices are on the updates page, read from our licensing service.
